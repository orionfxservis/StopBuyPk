Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about comparing prices and saving big with StopBuyPk.

What is StopBuyPk?

StopBuyPk is Pakistan's premier smart price comparison platform. We help users find the best prices, compare deals across local stores, track market trends, and make informed shopping decisions for food, groceries, electronics, and more.

How does the price comparison work?

We monitor and aggregate prices from verified online and physical stores in Pakistan. Our platform compares these prices in real-time, highlighting the cheapest offers, price drops, and special discounts.

Is StopBuyPk free to use?

Absolutely! StopBuyPk is 100% free for all shoppers. We do not charge any membership fees or add extra commission on purchases.

How do I order on WhatsApp?

When you find the best deal, click "Order on WhatsApp". This automatically creates a pre-formatted message with order details that you can send directly to the shop/seller to confirm your purchase.

Can I list my shop on StopBuyPk?

Yes! Retailers and local shops can register to show their catalog on StopBuyPk. Contact our vendor support team to list your store.
